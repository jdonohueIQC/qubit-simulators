Correlation E
State vectors, operators, and probabilities
Alice’s operators act only on the first qubit (M ⊗ I) and Bob’s only on the second (I ⊗ M), so the two sides commute — the order shown above never changes a probability. A projector that blocks a qubit still counts as a measurement, so it collapses the partner too. Correlation E = P(same) − P(different), taken over pairs where both qubits reach a measurement. Alice’s own 0/1 statistics (counting every pair, whatever happens to Bob’s qubit) never depend on Bob’s choices — try it.
CHSH: with W = (Z+X)/√2 and V = (Z−X)/√2, record E for Alice ∈ {Z, X} and Bob ∈ {W, V}. Any local hidden-variable story obeys |E(Z,W) + E(Z,V) + E(X,W) − E(X,V)| ≤ 2; the state |Φ+⟩ reaches 2√2 ≈ 2.83, while the classically correlated ρcorr stays at √2 ≈ 1.41.
